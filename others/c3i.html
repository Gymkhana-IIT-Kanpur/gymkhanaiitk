About C3i Hub

Overview

The Interdisciplinary Centre for Cyber Security and Cyber Defence of Critical Infrastructure (C3i Hub) was established at IIT Kanpur in 2018, under the framework of the National Mission on Cyber Security and Cyber Defence (NMCSD). The centre is a unique initiative aimed at carrying out R&D, education, training, and technology incubation in cyber security, blockchain, and IoT security.

C3i Hub has been a trailblazer in India's cyber security ecosystem, providing solutions to various sectors including defence, energy, transportation, and finance.

Vision & Mission

  • To build a cyber-resilient India through research and innovation.
  • To develop indigenous cyber security and blockchain technologies.
  • To create a skilled workforce through education and training.
  • To incubate cyber security startups.

Areas of Research

  • Cyber Security & Cyber Defence
  • Blockchain & Distributed Ledger Technology
  • IoT & Critical Infrastructure Security
  • Artificial Intelligence for Security
  • Cryptography & Privacy

Partners & Collaborators

  • National Mission on Cyber Security and Cyber Defence (NMCSD)
  • Ministry of Electronics and IT (MeitY), Government of India
  • Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)
  • Various Industry Partners
